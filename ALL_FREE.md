# Free Rider — All Free Policy

Free Rider is built around one rule: **every official Free Rider feature is available to everyone for free.**

The name “Free Rider” is intentional. The official project is both free to use and open source.

## What All Free means

- No Pro, Premium, Team, Business, or Enterprise feature tier.
- No paid-only features, feature flags, usage limits, or artificial product restrictions intended to force an upgrade.
- Official desktop releases and official project features remain free of charge.
- The source code stays publicly available and community contributions are welcome.
- Optional donations, sponsorships, or paid support may fund the project, but they must not unlock product functionality unavailable to everyone else.

## License

Free Rider is licensed under **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (`AGPL-3.0-only`)**.

The AGPL keeps Free Rider and modified versions open when they are distributed, and also requires source availability for modified versions offered to users over a network.

The license itself permits commercial use and charging money for copies or services. “All Free” is an additional policy for the **official Free Rider project and releases**, not a restriction added to the open-source license.
