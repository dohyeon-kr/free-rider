## All Free policy

Separate from the license, the **official Free Rider project and official releases keep all product features free for everyone**.

- No paid-only product features
- No artificial feature locks or usage caps intended to force an upgrade
- Official desktop releases remain free of charge
- Source code stays public
- Donations, sponsorships, or paid support may fund the project, but they do not unlock exclusive product functionality

See the repository's [All Free Policy](https://github.com/dohyeon-kr/free-rider/blob/main/ALL_FREE.md) for the complete policy.
